# Unsent NARA reference enquiry

Prepared on October 3, 2026.
No enquiry has been sent or copy order placed.
The original research scope excludes external enquiries; sending requires separate authorization.
The [official College Park page](https://www.archives.gov/college-park) lists `archives2reference@nara.gov` for Textual Consultation; this address was checked on October 3, 2026.
The item identifiers below come from the [public RG 457 finding aid](https://www.maritime.org/doc/nara/nsaopendoor.php) and need confirmation against current holdings.

To: archives2reference@nara.gov

Subject: RG 457 reference enquiry: May 1945 Thetis naval Enigma key material

Dear Textual Reference staff,

I am researching an undeciphered German naval radiogram dated May 1, 1945, [published as P1030680](https://enigma.hoerenberg.com/index.php?cat=Unbroken&page=P1030680).
Its working attribution is to Schlüssel M Thetis, a Baltic U-boat exercise network; that attribution still needs a dated allocation list.
[R.I.P.
401, Figure 24b, printed 92](https://cryptocellar.org/enigma/files/nr-4457-rip-401.pdf#page=68), also describes the earlier name U-Uebungsschluessel.

Could you confirm the current references and availability of these items from the public RG 457 Historic Cryptographic Collection finding aid?

- Box 37, NR 201, former CBBA55, ACNR 4775A: German Cipher Book and Related Key Sheets, 1944–1945.
- Box 37, NR 202, former CBBA55, ACNR 4776A: German Cipher Key Tables for Ciphers F, M, N and a Manual Emergency Cipher, 1941–1945.
- Box 620, NR 1665, former CBKJ17, ACNR 7465A: German Key Logs, February 1941–May 1945.
- Boxes 38–39, NR 205, former CBBA56, ACNR 4811A: Allocation Lists for Discriminants and Call Signs 4 and 5.

For NR 201/202, I would be grateful for any existing folder list or scan identifying Thetis material for April 30–May 2, 1945, or a May monthly sheet and validity notice.
The relevant items would include inner wheel/ring settings, Allgemein and Offizier outer plug settings, Grund-setting tables, and any accompanying key-change instructions.
Material labeled Potsdam, Hydra or another network would require a separate applicability rule before it could be used for this message.

For NR 1665, does the item contain machine key settings as well as daily key-breaking dates and times, and is Thetis included?
Colin Burke's [2011 study](https://userpages.umbc.edu/~burke/raisinglong1-2011.pdf), PDF pages 9 and 27, cites it for Shark key-breaking times and the Allied center that first solved each key.
I have not examined the logs themselves.

For NR 205, does the item include the dated Hering allocation list or amendments effective in March–May 1945?
The published reconstruction associates ACH with column 645, but the photographed page lacks a complete identifying header; I need to establish the effective edition rather than assume that an older allocation applies.

Links to existing digital copies or folder descriptions would be especially helpful.
If reproduction would be necessary, please advise the scope and estimated cost before any order is placed.

Thank you for your help.
