## Input and key allocation

The two indicator readings, VROL NMKA and VAOL NMKA, are retained independently of the body.

Its visible portion does not establish its edition or effective date.
The publisher's identification supports Thetis as the principal hypothesis, subject to confirming the dated list and daily-key interval.

The published May 1 Potsdam key reproduces OEDM → ELKC at MNNS, SEDM → PUYY at DGUG, and JCRSAJ → IPZAYK at PUYY under physical rings VCCH.
These exact replay checks support [the publisher's reconstruction](https://enigma.hoerenberg.com/index.php?cat=Unbroken&page=P1030680); they do not recover the Thetis message key.
No verified rule permits transferring these Grund settings, wheel orders, plugs, or rings to Thetis.
The [published daily keys](https://enigma.hoerenberg.com/index.php?cat=The+U534+messages&page=The+messages) include May 1, but belong to Potsdam; sharing a calendar date does not establish sharing a key.

The CLI and externally supplied language judge remain in place.
GPU tests now fail if a hardware adapter cannot be opened; CPU renderer adapters are rejected.
An explicit `bombe --gpu` cannot silently fall back to CPU, while `--plan` needs no GPU.
