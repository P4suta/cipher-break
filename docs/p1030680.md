## Further primary-source review

[The constraint register](../data/p1030680/constraints.json) records the additional sources, reviewed pages, file hashes and their consequences for the search.

The recovered 568/AAEL, 438/AACU and 528/AAFB keys in [the publisher's list](https://enigma.hoerenberg.com/index.php?cat=The+U534+messages&page=The+messages) belong to 30 April, 1 May and 2 May 1945, respectively, on Potsdam.
Their common rightmost wheel VIII cannot be treated as agreement among three independent May 1 nets.
It supplies no verified Thetis wheel restriction.

[The original Kenngruppenbuch, copy 992](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/m4/files/KBuch_1941_992_q50.pdf), printed VIII–IX, distinguishes the key-net and procedure groups and requires consulting the current allotment list.
PDF page 116 contains a Hering amendment label citing order MND IIc 2063/43 of 11 February 1943, entered on 19 April 1943.
The shared code word Hering therefore does not by itself identify the target's 1945 allocation edition.
PDF page 115 prints Thetis at columns 621–653 but crosses it out by hand and adds another name; the amendment date is not established.
The unannotated publisher photograph still needs matching to the effective table and validity notice.

[M.Dv.32/3, amended copy 62](https://www.cryptomuseum.com/crypto/enigma/m4/files/Schluessel_M_Allgemeine_Bestimmungen_1941_62.pdf), printed 33 and 34a, specifies different key-change times: 1200 DGZ for Potsdam in section 153, and 0700 DGZ for Thetis in section 157.
The latter section assigns Thetis to tactical U-boat exercises and describes Allgemein and Offizier sheets normally issued monthly.
Monthly issue does not mean a single monthly machine setting.
Both leaves are Deckblatt 8; the reviewed copy does not establish its effective date or continued applicability in May 1945.
The target's unresolved minutes, the key-sheet interval and any later orders must be checked before selecting a daily entry.

Mahon's official [History of Hut Eight, printed page 108](https://www.alanturing.net/turing_archive/archive/a/A09/A09-110.gif), records deteriorating wheel-order rules in February–March 1945 and unrestricted Greek-wheel ring positions spreading to Bonito and Plaice in March.
Older wheel-list habits and fixed Greek rings cannot be imposed on Thetis from that evidence.

Printed [page 96](https://www.alanturing.net/turing_archive/archive/a/A09/A09-098.gif) identifies Plaice as the expanded Laube/Potsdam Baltic key introduced in January 1944.

These passages were checked against the original images after searching the public Mahon and Alexander transcriptions.

The lack of a Thetis name in those searched transcriptions is not proof that no wartime key record survives.
